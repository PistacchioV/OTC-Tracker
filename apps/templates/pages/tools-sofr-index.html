Interest period

The shift moves the whole window — dates and weights. The lookback moves only the rate reading inside it. Both zero, the calculation observes the period itself; both equal, it is a lookback with observation shift. Usual values: 2 to 5 business days.

Formula

factor = Π (1 + ri · ni/360)
rate   = (factor − 1) · 360/D

ni are the calendar days until the next business day — Friday's fixing earns three days.
