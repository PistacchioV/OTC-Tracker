Reads the raw CETIP files from the B3 daily download folder, renames them to the standard convention and saves them into the settlement folders used by the KPI. The TO of each distribution e-mail is editable below (saved for the next runs); OTC Ops stays in CC.

    Forecasts & Reports

    Settlement Reporting

    Rotinas que consolidam posições e geram relatórios analíticos enviados por e-mail.

    Settlement Forecast

    Reads the CETIP position files, projects the upcoming settlements by business day and entity, and e-mails the forecast report. Set the TO / CC once — they are saved and reused on every run.

    CETIP positions → e-mail

    Update Contacts

    Imports the "CONTATO DE CLIENTES" spreadsheet and replaces each counterparty's contacts with the Active ones only (matched by SPN, leading zeros ignored). CGD and banking data are preserved.

    Spreadsheet → Counterparties

    Daily Metric — Outstanding Confirmation Brazil OTC

    Builds the Outstanding Confirmation Brazil OTC daily metric as an Outlook draft (.eml download) addressed to the saved recipient list — open the file to review and send it yourself. Set the recipients (TO) once — they are saved and reused on every run. The reference date is always today.

    Saved recipients → .eml draft

    Pending Confirmation — Weekly Escalation (CEM/EDG)

    Weekly (Friday) escalation of confirmations pending > 30 days, split by LOB (CEM / EDG), grouped by banker and broken down by company. Builds it as an Outlook draft (.eml download) addressed to the saved recipients — open the file to review and send it yourself. Set the TO / CC once — they are saved and reused on every run.

    CEM/EDG > 30 days → .eml draft

    Pending Signature Confirmations — Collection

    Builds one editable e-mail draft per counterparty for confirmations pending signature (Pending Digital Signature / Pending Original), segregated from the Pending Confirmation base. TO = the counterparty's confirmation contacts; CC = that counterparty's bankers + Brazil OTC Ops + IS Trade Doc. Downloads a .zip of .eml drafts — nothing is sent automatically.

    Loading…
    One .eml per counterparty → .zip

    Deals Monitor — Pending Action

    Sent automatically at 19:00 and 19:30 with every Deals Monitor card that still has deals outside Success — by type (Registration / Confirmation / Intrag), product and detail. Nothing pending, no e-mail. Set the TO / CC once — they are saved and reused on every run.

    Daily 19:00 & 19:30 → e-mail

    Pending Confirmations Spreadsheet Metrics

    Saves the "PENDING - Outstanding Confirmation OTC.xlsx" spreadsheet to the Movimento share, overwriting the previous file — the Pending rows of the Pending Confirmation page, in the historical spreadsheet layout, with date columns as real dates (dd/mm/yyyy). Runs automatically every ANBIMA business day at 10:45 Brasília (7:15 PM IST); Run saves it now.

    Today saves the current situation. An earlier date rebuilds the spreadsheet from that day's pending snapshot and overwrites the same file on the share, so the global team can pull it from the usual path — run it again with today's date to put the file back.
    Daily 10:45 BRT → .xlsx on share

    BACC EA Metrics

    E-mails the manual operations of the Track Confirmations page as an .xlsx attachment, with the columns the BACC metrics team consolidates. Runs automatically every ANBIMA business day at 16:00 Brasília; Run sends it now. An empty spreadsheet is still sent — a day with no manual operation is itself the metric.

    Daily 16:00 BRT → e-mail + .xlsx

    Confirmations Escalation

    E-mails the confirmations still pending validation on the Confirmations Monitor / Track Confirmations. Routine: Monday and Thursday at 17:00 Brasília (rolled to the next ANBIMA business day) — one report per desk: OTC Ops (Pending OTC), Sales Support (Pending MO) and one per product for the Front Office, each with its own recipient list. Escalation: every business day, only what is on the last day of the MO deadline or already overdue. Nothing pending, no e-mail. Each item can also be sent on its own with its Run button.

    Manual Deals EA

    E-mails the deals closed manually so the automatic EA does not consider them and the metrics team counts them apart. Two routines, each with its own reference date: NDF Other Publisher runs at 20:00 Brasília with the deals of the day itself (external counterparties only), and NDF FWD Start runs at 16:30 on each deal's Strike Set Date, carrying the Deal Id of the vanilla re-booking. With no deal to report, no e-mail is sent.

    Other Publisher 20:00  ·  FWD Start 16:30 BRT

    MT300

    E-mails the day's NDF Vanilla trades whose counterparty is registered in Mapping › MT300, so the group can match them in DVP. Runs every ANBIMA business day at 19:30 Brasília; Run sends it now. With no trade from the registered counterparties, no e-mail is sent.

    Daily 19:30 BRT → NDF Vanilla
